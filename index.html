PMO & Process Optimization
Project Management Infrastructure

OPO builds the systems and processes that let your business handle more projects, better, without becoming reactive. SOP development, platform implementation, AI-driven automation, and document control, designed to scale.

  • SOP development: sales intake, order management, QA/QC, shipping, and client handover
  • Platform and software implementation: project tracking, milestone management, client-facing reporting
  • AI process automation: identify and implement tools to eliminate bottlenecks and improve operational visibility
  • Organizational scaling infrastructure: onboarding documentation and role frameworks
  • Document control: centralized filing for SOPs, compliance records, and project files
  • ISO 9001:2015 QMS development and implementation
02
Owner Representation
Independent Project Oversight

OPO acts as your eyes and ears throughout the project lifecycle, from specification review before anything is ordered, through manufacturing and testing, to commissioning and handover. Independent, informed, and accountable to you.

  • Specification and drawing review before procurement
  • Manufacturing oversight during production
  • Factory acceptance test witnessing before equipment ships
  • Shipping and logistics coordination
  • Site commissioning support and energization oversight
  • Independent client reporting throughout delivery
  • Available for power transformers, switchgear, and critical industrial equipment
03
Safety & Compliance
Safety Implementation & Audit Services

OPO delivers occupational health and safety program development, ISO lead auditing, and compliance implementation for industrial operations. Building safety and quality management systems that protect your people and your business.

  • Occupational health and safety program development
  • Safety management system (SMS) implementation
  • ISO 45001:2018 Occupational Health & Safety management system implementation
  • ISO lead audit services: gap assessments, internal audits, and pre-certification readiness
  • ISO 9001:2015 QMS implementation and audit support
  • Hazard identification, risk assessment, and control frameworks
  • Regulatory compliance review and documentation
  • Site safety inspection and reporting

Safety & Compliance

ISO 45001:2018 & OHS Implementation

ISO 45001:2018 is the international standard for occupational health and safety management systems. OPO implements, audits, and prepares industrial operations for certification, building the frameworks that protect your workforce and demonstrate due diligence to regulators and clients.

  • Gap analysis against ISO 45001:2018 requirements
  • OHS management system documentation and implementation
  • Internal audit and management review facilitation
  • Hazard identification and risk control frameworks (hierarchy of controls)
  • Pre-certification readiness and corrective action support
  • Ongoing compliance monitoring and regulatory alignment
