Two Pillars

One integrated
engagement.

OPO operates across two distinct practice areas that work together. Whether your business needs operational infrastructure, project delivery expertise, or a trusted representative sitting on the owner's side of a major equipment purchase, OPO brings both disciplines under one engagement.

01
Pillar One
PMO and Process Optimization

OPO embeds into your operations to build the project management infrastructure and process systems your business needs to scale without becoming reactive. This is not advisory work. OPO implements, documents, and operates the function.

  • SOP development across sales, order management, supplier communication, QA/QC, shipping, and client handover
  • Project management platform setup, templates, milestone tracking, and client-facing reporting
  • Lean Six Sigma process improvement and waste elimination
  • AI process automation to reduce manual work and improve operational visibility
  • ISO 9001:2015 quality management system development and audit readiness
  • Onboarding documentation, role frameworks, and organizational scaling infrastructure
  • Document control systems and centralized filing for compliance records
  • Monthly operations reviews with leadership on performance and priorities
02
Pillar Two
Owner Representation on Industrial Projects

OPO acts as the owner's representative on transformer, electrical infrastructure, and capital industrial equipment projects. With direct manufacturing experience from inside a transformer facility, OPO knows what to ask, what to inspect, and where projects break down before they do.

  • Pre-purchase scope review, specification alignment, and vendor evaluation
  • Drawing review and engineering submittal management on behalf of the owner
  • Manufacturing oversight and progress monitoring at the supplier facility
  • Factory Acceptance Testing witnessing and documentation
  • Shipping coordination, logistics oversight, and damage risk management
  • Site commissioning oversight and energization support
  • Client-facing project status reporting throughout delivery
  • Warranty period monitoring and issue escalation on behalf of the owner

Owner Representation

Your interests.
Protected.

Most owners purchase complex electrical or industrial equipment without anyone in their corner who truly understands what happens inside the manufacturer's facility. OPO closes that gap by providing experienced, independent oversight from specification through to energization.

01
Specification and Vendor Selection

OPO reviews technical specifications, assesses vendor proposals, and helps owners select the right supplier based on capability, quality history, and commercial terms rather than price alone.

02
Manufacturing Oversight and FAT

OPO monitors production milestones, reviews drawing submittals, and witnesses Factory Acceptance Testing to verify the equipment meets specification before it ships. Issues caught at FAT cost a fraction of issues caught in the field.

03
Delivery and Site Commissioning

OPO coordinates shipping logistics, monitors delivery risk, and provides oversight during site installation and commissioning to ensure the equipment is energized correctly and the project closes cleanly.

04
Reporting and Issue Escalation

OPO provides structured project status reporting throughout the delivery cycle and escalates non-conformances, schedule delays, or quality concerns directly on the owner's behalf.
